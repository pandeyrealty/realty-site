Common questions

Good to know

What's the difference between pre-qualification and pre-approval?

A pre-qualification is an estimate based on what you tell a lender. A pre-approval involves reviewing your credit and documents. Neither is a final loan approval; that comes after underwriting and an acceptable appraisal on a specific property.

How much do I need for a down payment?

It depends on the program. Some conventional loans for qualifying buyers allow as little as 3% down, FHA loans can allow 3.5% down for qualifying borrowers, and VA loans can allow no down payment for eligible veterans and service members. You'll also need funds for closing costs and, sometimes, reserves.

Does checking my options hurt my credit?

Using the calculators on this site doesn't involve your credit at all. A full pre-approval usually includes a credit check. Ask us before you apply if you'd like to talk through timing.

Can I use rent from a duplex or rental to qualify?

Often, part of the expected rental income can be counted, depending on the program and documentation such as leases or an appraiser's rent estimate. The rules differ between loan types, so it's worth a quick conversation.

Why does Pandey Realty offer both real estate and mortgage?

So your search and your financing move on one timeline with one team accountable for both. You're never required to use both services.
