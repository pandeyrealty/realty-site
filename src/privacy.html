Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026

This policy explains how Pandey Realty, Inc. ("Pandey Realty," "we," "us") collects and uses information through pandey.realty and our property websites.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us through contact, showing, and inquiry forms: your name, email, phone number, preferred showing times, and your message.
  • Basic technical information that web servers record automatically, such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, used to operate and secure the site.

Our website forms are not designed to collect Social Security numbers, account numbers, or loan documents. Please don't send those through the site. Mortgage applications are completed through a separate, secure application platform with its own privacy notice.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry, schedule showings, and provide the real estate or mortgage services you request.
  • To contact you by phone, text, or email about your inquiry, when you've agreed to that on our forms. You can ask us to stop at any time.
  • To keep records required by law and to protect the site from abuse.

Sharing

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate (such as our website host and email provider), with parties involved in a transaction you ask us to help with, or when required by law.

Calculators

Numbers you type into our calculators stay in your browser and are not sent to us.

Your California privacy rights

California residents may request to know, correct, or delete personal information we hold about them, subject to legal exceptions. To make a request, email pandeyrealty7@gmail.com or call (469) 407-5853.
